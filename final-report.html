What happened, in plain words

We gave responders less time to answer,
then punished them for not answering in time.

⏱️
Step 1

Less time to answer

A job buzzes a responder's phone. The update cut their time to answer from 90 seconds to 60.

📵
Step 2

More jobs slipped past

Overnight, unanswered jobs jumped from about 1 in 40 to nearly 1 in 5.

📉
Step 3

Treated as a "no", forever

A slow answer counted the same as saying no. Each one pushed them down the queue, with no way back.

By the numbers

Small change, big consequences

1 in 5jobs went unanswered in time after the update. It was 1 in 40.
12 → 0jobs a week offered to Farlight, July to end of August
4responders pushed out of their own areas
45complaints after the update, and none before it

It wasn't the summer slowdown. Summer explains fewer jobs overall, not people missing them, and September brought no recovery.

Why it got worse, not better

A trap with no way out

Every missed buzz moved them further back in the queue. Further back meant fewer jobs, and fewer jobs meant fewer chances to win their place back.

These four weren't slower or further away. Before the update they were as busy as anyone.
